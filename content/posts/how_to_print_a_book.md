I recently got my hands on the PDF of a book i was planning on reading and i had a whole load of university printing quota that i needed to finish off before the semester (printing period) officially ends. And what's reading a book if not physically flipping through each page and so i decided to print out this book

Sounds pretty simple(it kind of is), but it's easy to mess up and when you're tight on how many test runs you've got, might as well not waste any pages.

My goal wasn’t just to print the PDF, I wanted something closer to an actual book. What I wanted was a binded form of the book and I quickly realised printing one page per page would very quickly turn a 400 page book into an absolute cinderblock. 

So I went with:
- **2 pages per side**
- **Double-sided printing**

Which effectively gives `4 book pages per sheet of paper`
So `400 pages -> 100 sheets`

Much more reasonable.

I also wanted the reading experience to feel natural. I really didn’t want to have to rotate the entire book every time I flipped a page


So here’s the exact setup I ended up using, nothing fancy, just what actually works.


### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Open the PDF in **Adobe Acrobat (Reader or Pro)**
2. Go to `File → Print`

### Page Sizing & Handling

- Select **Multiple**
- Set `Pages per sheet: 2 by 1`
- Set `Page order: Horizontal`

### Orientation

- Select **Landscape**

### Optional but Recommended

- Enable **Print page border** (helps prevent the pages from looking too small)
- Enable **Print on both sides of paper**
- Under **Flip on**: choose **Long edge**

### Advanced Settings

- Click `Properties` or `Advanced`
- Under **Quality tab**:
  - Set `Print quality: 1200 DPI`
- Under **Basic tab**:
  - Set `Zoom: 100%`  
    *(Do not use 110%, it's too much and may cut edges)*

---

## Testing Instructions

Before printing the entire document:

1. Decide what pages you want printed as a book and what in full size
   - You may want the cover page to be in full size, so you decide that you want to print only pages `2-441` ( thats 440 total pages to print)
1. Print a test run with just the **first 4 pages**
   - Set `Pages: 2–5`
1. Check:
   - Both front and back print correctly
   - Pages are in correct **left-to-right** order
   - No clipping or margin issues
   - Duplex flips on the correct side

3. Adjust settings if needed, then print the full document

---

### Final Checks Before Printing

- Confirm layout in **Preview pane**
- Ensure the total number of **book pages is a multiple of 4**
  - If not, you will have blank pages
- Confirm duplex (double-sided) printing is enabled with **flip on long edge**


## Important Reminders

- Always print in **multiples of 4** pages (e.g., 428 ✅, 429 ❌ unless padded)
- Use **Preview** before printing the full job
- Test different **zoom levels** (e.g., 102%, 104%) for margin fit
- Consider trimming PDF whitespace using a crop tool
